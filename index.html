        Key lesson

        Spinal cord cross-section

        Lower motor-neuron cell bodies lie in the anterior (ventral) horn of the gray matter. Their axons leave the cord through the ventral root and travel within peripheral nerves to reach skeletal muscle. (By contrast, UPPER motor neurons live in the brain's motor cortex and send their axons down the cord — ALS can affect both groups; this page focuses on the lower motor neuron.)
