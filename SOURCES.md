# Educational source map

This page is a software illustration for learning. It is deliberately
simplified, schematic, and not to scale; it is not medical advice and does not
provide diagnosis or treatment guidance. It does not implement a clinical staging system.
The timeline labels are teaching states that organize the concepts shown in the
animation; they are not a universal order of events for people with ALS.

| Topic in the visualization | Where it appears | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upper and lower motor neurons, the ventral horn and the motor unit | Header note, spinal-cord cross-section, labels and key lesson | [NCBI Bookshelf: Lower Motor Neuron Circuits and Motor Control](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK10979/) |
| Motor unit structure and skeletal-muscle activation | Motor-unit label, signal journey and comparison panel | [NCBI Bookshelf: Physiology, Skeletal Muscle Contraction](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK559006/) |
| Neuromuscular-junction anatomy, acetylcholine and transmission to muscle | Neuromuscular-junction and acetylcholine labels | [NCBI Bookshelf: Physiology, Neuromuscular Transmission](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK541133/) |
| Axonal transport as a bidirectional process and its study in motor-neuron disease | Axonal-transport label and transport animation | [PubMed: Neurobiology of axonal transport defects in motor neuron diseases](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28235672/) |
| Denervation, collateral reinnervation and motor-unit loss | Denervation, reinnervation, atrophy and motor-unit teaching states | [Assessment of Motor Units in Neuromuscular Disease](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5233620/) |
| ALS affects motor neurons and has no single diagnostic test | Disclaimer and upper/lower motor-neuron note | [NINDS: Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS)](https://www.ninds.nih.gov/health-information/disorders/amyotrophic-lateral-sclerosis-als) |

The denervation/reinnervation reference is a general neuromuscular review that
includes ALS; it does not validate this animation's order, timing or clinical
interpretation. All references support concepts, not the exact visual timing,
shapes, colors or simplified state transitions. This source map is an editorial
reference record; it does not certify medical accuracy or turn the illustration
into a clinical resource.
